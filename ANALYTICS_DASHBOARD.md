# Analytics Dashboard

Read-only dashboard added to the Analytics page.

It displays:
- videos with analytics
- total views
- average CTR
- average percentage viewed
- average AVD
- total watch time
- subscribers
- registered videos
- per-video performance diagnosis
- top videos
- publish-day summary

Diagnosis logic is relative to current channel medians:
- STRONG = CTR and APV both at/above channel median
- PACKAGING OPPORTUNITY = CTR below median, APV at/above median
- CONTENT HOLD OPPORTUNITY = CTR at/above median, APV below median
- PACKAGING + CONTENT = both below median

No titles, scripts, Medical Gates, Production files, or analytics records are modified by the dashboard.
